CyberHawks Fledglings is our highschool outreach program.

## Fledglings CTF

The University of North Georgia is hosting a beginner friendly Capture the Flag for teams of highschool students.

**When:**
* November 21st
* Time is still under development: Expect 8-10 hours

**Where:**
* The University of North Georgia
* [The Mike Cottrell College of Business](https://ung.edu/mike-cottrell-college-of-business/index.php)
* 256 S. Chestatee St., Dahlonega GA, 30533

**Who:**
* Any highschooler is eligible to participate
* This is also a great opportunity for highschool faculty to network with UNG and develop their cyber programs
* Teams can range from 1 to 4 people (full teams of 4 are preferred)

**What:**
* Challenge categories will include OSINT, Reverse Engineering, Password Cracking, Forensics, and potentially a Full PWN machine
* The event will be hosted on CTFD

**Required Hardware:**
* Students are encouraged to bring their own laptops with Kali Linux Virtual Machines
* There will be one NETLAB VM allocated per team that students can connect to with their chromebooks if they do not have laptops available
* Please reach out if you are concerned about hardware being your limitation for competing

**Registration:**
* Have a faculty member reach out to Caitlin.Conneely@ung.edu
* Registration ends on October 31st

**Additional Information**
* Questions: Reach out to james.goble@ung.edu and CC Caitlin.Conneely@ung.edu
* Official Rules Packet: In Development
* There is no cost for attending this event
* Training recommendations: [Linux Luminarium](https://pwn.college/linux-luminarium/), [Pico CTF](https://picoctf.org)
* Coach Clarification: 
    * Each team will be expected to provide a faculty member to serve as a coach for this event
    * Teams from the same institution may use the same coach
    * Parents are acceptable as coaches if no faculty member is available
    * The purpose of this is to strengthen ties with Georgia highschools moving forward
    * Please reach out to Caitlin if you are unable to find an applicable coach
